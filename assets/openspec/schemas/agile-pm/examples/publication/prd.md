# Product Increment: Join A Writing Group

## Hypothesis And Scope

An invited writer can choose whether to join a group and understand why a stale
invitation cannot grant access. This fictional example approves no real delivery.
Automatic joining, access escalation, and public discovery are excluded.

## Capability PRD Index

| Capability path | Kind | Product page | Priority | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [invitations](prd/capabilities/invitations.md) | user | capabilities/invitations.md | Must | Let a writer accept or decline a valid invitation |
| [access-control](prd/capabilities/access-control.md) | system | system-capabilities/access-control.md | Must | Grant only the valid invitation's intended access |

## Feedback Loop

Review acceptance, decline, and invalid-invitation cases against membership state.
Any changed outcome requires revising this increment and the proposed pages before
fresh approval; archive must not invent a result or mark the example shipped.
