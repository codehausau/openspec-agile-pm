# Product Documentation

The [product requirements overview](prd.md) introduces the cumulative product
documentation and indexes its capabilities and system capabilities. Detailed
requirements, journeys, flows, and acceptance outcomes live in those pages.
The `agile-pm` archive workflow publishes the reviewed set after delivery
reconciliation; approval alone does not update this handbook.

It describes product intent, user outcomes, requirements, priorities, and boundaries.
Delivery status and evidence are stated separately from approved intent.
This document is not by itself proof that behavior is available in a deployed version.

Proposed revisions and approval records live in active OpenSpec changes and move to
`openspec/changes/archive/` on archive. Earlier approvals revised within the same
change are retained in its `product-history/` directory. Revise the product set
through approval and archive; do not edit published pages directly.

## Historical PRD Sets

Older dated directories, if present, are historical snapshots from the previous
archive-publication workflow. Preserve their files and existing catalog rows.
New archives update the cumulative pages and MkDocs navigation without creating
dated product copies or adding rows to this historical catalog.
