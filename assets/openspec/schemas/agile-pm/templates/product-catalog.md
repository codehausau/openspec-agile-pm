# Product Documentation

The [master product requirements document](prd.md) is the single, cohesive source
of current approved product intent. The `agile-pm` approval workflow updates it
with the exact product revision reviewed and approved by the product owner.

It describes product intent, user outcomes, requirements, priorities, and boundaries.
Approval precedes implementation: delivery status and evidence are stated separately.
This document is not by itself proof that behavior is available in a deployed version.

Proposed revisions and approval records live in active OpenSpec changes and move to
`openspec/changes/archive/` on archive. Earlier approvals revised within the same
change are retained in its `product-history/` directory. Revise the master through
the approval workflow; do not edit the published file directly.

## Historical PRD Sets

Older dated directories, if present, are historical snapshots from the previous
archive-publication workflow. Preserve their files and existing catalog rows.
New approvals update the master; new archives do not create dated product copies.
